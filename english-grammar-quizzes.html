Why study grammar through examples?

Grammar rules become easier to use when learners see them in complete sentences. Instead of memorising a definition alone, identify the word's function, notice its position in the sentence, and then create a similar sentence of your own.

  • Identify the grammatical category first.
  • Read the example sentence and ask what the target word is doing.
  • Compare similar forms that are commonly confused.
  • Use practice questions to test recall without looking at the answer.

Common grammar study mistakes

One common mistake is learning a rule without learning when it applies. Another is treating every word ending in -ly as an adverb or assuming that one word always belongs to the same grammatical category. Context matters. Good practice therefore combines definitions with complete sentences.
