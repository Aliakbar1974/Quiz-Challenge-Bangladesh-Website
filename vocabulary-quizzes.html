How to learn vocabulary effectively

Knowing a translation is only the first step. A useful vocabulary item should also be recognised in context and used correctly in a sentence. When you meet a new word, learn its meaning, part of speech, pronunciation if relevant, common combinations, and at least one natural example.

  • Meaning: understand the central meaning rather than memorising a single translation.
  • Context: notice how the word behaves in a real sentence.
  • Recall: hide the answer and try to retrieve it from memory.
  • Use: create your own sentence after learning the word.
Study tip: Review difficult words again after a gap. Spaced retrieval is more useful than reading the same word repeatedly in one sitting.

Meaning is not enough

Words often have several meanings or different levels of formality. For example, a word may be positive in one context and neutral in another, or it may commonly appear with particular verbs or nouns. That is why our vocabulary practice aims to show explanations and examples alongside the correct answer whenever the quiz format supports them.
