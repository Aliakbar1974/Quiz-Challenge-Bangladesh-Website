Adverbs: Learn the Rule, Then Practice

An adverb gives extra information about an action or description. It can tell us how, when, where, how often, or to what degree something happens.

MannerShe speaks softly.
TimeHe arrived yesterday.
PlaceCome here.
FrequencyShe always studies.
DegreeIt is very useful.
