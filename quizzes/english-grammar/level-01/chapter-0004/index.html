Adjectives: Learn How Words Describe Nouns

An adjective describes or gives more information about a noun or pronoun. It can show quality, size, number, possession, comparison, or other characteristics.

For example, in “She bought a red dress,” the word red describes the noun “dress.” Adjectives become easier to understand when you identify exactly which noun or pronoun they modify.

How to use this chapter
