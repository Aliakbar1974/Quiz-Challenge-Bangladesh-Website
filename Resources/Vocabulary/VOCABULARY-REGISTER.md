# Vocabulary Library — Master Duplicate Register

This is the working control list for the 7,000-item Vocabulary Library. Before publishing any new word or phrasal verb, normalize its headword/phrase and compare it with every item below and all later entries added to this register.

## Duplicate-control rules

1. A word lemma is counted once. Inflected forms (for example, *give*, *gives*, *giving*) do not become separate entries unless they are genuinely different headwords with distinct dictionary treatment.
2. A phrasal verb is counted by its canonical phrase. Minor spelling, tense, pronoun or object variations do not create a new entry.
3. Do not publish a phrase again merely because it has another meaning. If one phrasal verb has several common senses, explain them under one entry.
4. Do not count an ordinary verb and a phrasal verb as duplicates automatically (for example, *look* and *look after* are different entries).
5. Preserve the original source wording in the provenance notes, but use a correct dictionary headword in the lesson where appropriate.
6. The two uploaded source documents overlap substantially. Cross-check both documents and the existing quiz/resource vocabulary before adding new items.
7. Do not assign a precise CEFR level unless the level is supported by a reliable learner-dictionary reference. Use an approximate label or omit it where uncertain.
