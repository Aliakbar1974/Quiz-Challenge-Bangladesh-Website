VOCABULARY RESOURCE · CHAPTER 01

Meaning, Context and Word Choice

Learn each word as a complete language item: pronunciation, grammatical role, meaning, word relationships and a sentence that shows how it is used.

Learning objectives

After studying this lesson, you should be able to explain the central meaning of each word, recognise its part of speech, understand its Bengali equivalent and use it in a suitable sentence.

Pronunciation note: IPA is shown as a practical guide. Pronunciation can vary between British and American English; use the linked dictionaries to hear the audio and compare accents.

1. Pensive

Adjective/ˈpen.sɪv/

বাংলা অর্থ: চিন্তামগ্ন, ভাবুক, গভীর চিন্তায় মগ্ন।

English meaning: Deeply thoughtful, often with a serious or slightly sad expression.

Synonyms: thoughtful, reflective, contemplative. Contrast: carefree, unreflective.

Example: She looked pensive while thinking about her future.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

2. Persuade

Verb/pəˈsweɪd/

বাংলা অর্থ: রাজি করানো, বোঝানো, কোনো কাজ বা মত গ্রহণে প্রভাবিত করা।

English meaning: To convince someone to do something or to believe or accept an idea.

Synonyms: convince, encourage, influence. Antonym: dissuade.

Example: She persuaded her brother to apply for the job.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

3. Pessimistic

Adjective/ˌpes.ɪˈmɪs.tɪk/

বাংলা অর্থ: হতাশাবাদী; যে ব্যক্তি খারাপ ফল বা নেতিবাচক পরিণতি প্রত্যাশা করে।

English meaning: Expecting bad results or tending to see the negative side of a situation.

Synonyms: gloomy, negative, doubtful. Antonym: optimistic.

Example: He is pessimistic about the company's future.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

4. Placate

Verb/pləˈkeɪt/

বাংলা অর্থ: রাগ বা অসন্তোষ কমানো, শান্ত করা, প্রশমিত করা।

English meaning: To calm someone who is angry or upset, sometimes by meeting a concern or demand.

Synonyms: appease, pacify, soothe. Antonym: provoke.

Example: The manager tried to placate the angry customer.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

5. Plea

Noun/pliː/

বাংলা অর্থ: আকুল আবেদন, আন্তরিক অনুরোধ; আইনগত ক্ষেত্রে আদালতে কোনো অভিযোগের জবাবে দেওয়া বক্তব্য।

English meaning: A serious or emotional request for help or action; in law, a formal statement by a defendant in response to a charge.

Related words: appeal, request, petition. There is no single direct antonym in every sense.

Example: The victim made a plea for justice.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

6. Plump

Adjective/plʌmp/

বাংলা অর্থ: গোলগাল, কিছুটা মোটাসোটা বা পূর্ণ আকৃতির।

English meaning: Having a pleasantly rounded or somewhat full shape.

Synonyms: rounded, chubby, fleshy. Contrast: slim, lean.

Example: The farmer raised several plump chickens.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

7. Ponder

Verb/ˈpɒn.dər/

বাংলা অর্থ: গভীরভাবে চিন্তা করা, ভেবে দেখা।

English meaning: To think about something carefully, especially before making a decision.

Synonyms: consider, contemplate, reflect on. Usage: ponder a question, ponder a decision.

Example: He sat quietly to ponder the difficult decision.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

8. Acrid

Adjective/ˈæk.rɪd/

বাংলা অর্থ: ঝাঁঝালো, তীব্র ও অস্বস্তিকর গন্ধ বা স্বাদযুক্ত।

English meaning: Having a strong, unpleasant smell or taste that can irritate the senses.

Synonyms: pungent, sharp, bitter. Contrast: mild, pleasant.

Example: An acrid smell filled the room after the fire.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

9. Arraign

Verb/əˈreɪn/

বাংলা অর্থ: ফৌজদারি অভিযোগের জবাব দেওয়ার জন্য অভিযুক্ত ব্যক্তিকে আদালতে হাজির করা।

English meaning: To bring an accused person before a court to answer a criminal charge.

Related legal terms: charge, indictment, court appearance. Contrast in outcome: acquit means to find someone not guilty; it is not a direct synonym.

Example: The accused was arraigned before the court.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

10. Contaminate

Verb/kənˈtæm.ɪ.neɪt/

বাংলা অর্থ: দূষিত করা; কোনো কিছুর মধ্যে ক্ষতিকর বা অনাকাঙ্ক্ষিত পদার্থ মিশিয়ে অপবিত্র করা।

English meaning: To make something impure, polluted or unsafe by adding an unwanted substance.

Synonyms: pollute, taint, infect (in suitable contexts). Antonym: purify.

Example: Chemicals can contaminate the drinking water.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

11. Contraction

Noun/kənˈtræk.ʃən/

বাংলা অর্থ: সংকোচন, সঙ্কুচিত হওয়া বা পরিমাণ/আকার কমে যাওয়া।

English meaning: A reduction in size, amount or length; in grammar, a shortened form such as do not → don't.

Antonym: expansion. Related forms: contract (verb), contracted (adjective/participle).

Example: The economy experienced a contraction last year.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

12. Cryptic

Adjective/ˈkrɪp.tɪk/

বাংলা অর্থ: রহস্যময়, দুর্বোধ্য, সহজে বোঝা যায় না এমন।

English meaning: Mysterious or difficult to understand because the meaning is hidden or unclear.

Synonyms: enigmatic, obscure, puzzling. Antonyms: clear, explicit, straightforward.

Example: He gave me a cryptic message before leaving.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

Quick review: distinguish similar ideas

WordRemember this distinction
PensiveA thoughtful state or expression; it does not simply mean sad.
PessimisticAn outlook that expects negative outcomes; its opposite is optimistic.
PersuadeTo convince someone; dissuade means to convince someone not to do something.
PlacateTo calm an upset person; it is not the same as agreeing with every demand.
PonderTo think carefully; it is more deliberate than simply noticing or remembering.
CrypticHard to understand because the meaning is hidden or unclear.

Self-check before taking the quiz

  1. Explain the difference between pensive and pessimistic.
  2. Write one sentence using persuade and another using placate.
  3. Which word describes a strong unpleasant smell: acrid or cryptic?
  4. Explain why contraction and expansion are opposites.
  5. Use ponder in a sentence about making an important decision.
