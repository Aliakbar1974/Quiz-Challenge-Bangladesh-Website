VOCABULARY RESOURCE · CHAPTER 02

Precision and Academic Vocabulary

These words appear in formal writing, public discussion, law, education and analytical contexts. Learn their precise meanings and notice the situations in which each word is appropriate.

Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to recognise these words in formal contexts, distinguish close meanings, and use them accurately in a sentence.

Study reminder: Some words have legal or formal meanings. Do not use a general synonym as if it were a complete legal definition. Check the dictionary entry when the context is specialised.

1. Forbearance

Noun/fɔːˈbeə.rəns/

বাংলা অর্থ: ধৈর্য, সংযম, বিরত থাকার মানসিক ক্ষমতা।

English meaning: Patient self-control or restraint; the act of refraining from reacting or acting.

Synonyms: patience, tolerance, restraint. Contrast: impatience.

Example: His forbearance prevented the argument from becoming more serious.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

2. Indictment

Noun/ɪnˈdaɪt.mənt/

বাংলা অর্থ: গুরুতর অপরাধের আনুষ্ঠানিক অভিযোগ; কোনো কিছুর তীব্র সমালোচনামূলক প্রমাণ।

English meaning: A formal accusation that someone has committed a serious crime; also, a statement or situation showing that something is wrong.

Related words: accusation, charge, allegation. Legal contrast: acquittal is a decision that a defendant is not guilty.

Example: The indictment charged him with several serious offences.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

3. Obsequious

Adjective/əbˈsiː.kwi.əs/

বাংলা অর্থ: অতিরিক্ত তোষামোদকারী, মন জোগাতে অতিরিক্ত আগ্রহী।

English meaning: Too eager to please or obey someone, especially in a way that seems servile.

Synonyms: servile, fawning, sycophantic. Antonyms: independent, self-assured.

Example: The obsequious employee agreed with everything his manager said.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

4. Phlegmatic

Adjective/fleɡˈmæt.ɪk/

বাংলা অর্থ: শান্ত, আবেগে সহজে বিচলিত হয় না এমন।

English meaning: Calm and unemotional, especially in a difficult or exciting situation.

Synonyms: composed, even-tempered, unflappable. Antonym: excitable.

Example: He remained phlegmatic even during the crisis.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

5. Posterity

Noun/pɒˈster.ə.ti/

বাংলা অর্থ: ভবিষ্যৎ প্রজন্ম, পরবর্তী বংশধররা।

English meaning: The people who will live in the future; future generations.

Related expression: generations to come. Contrast: ancestors refers to people from earlier generations.

Example: We must protect the environment for posterity.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

6. Precept

Noun/ˈpriː.sept/

বাংলা অর্থ: নীতি, আচরণবিধি, অনুসরণযোগ্য নির্দেশনা।

English meaning: A rule, principle or instruction intended to guide behaviour.

Synonyms: principle, guideline, rule, maxim.

Example: Honesty is an important precept of a good society.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

7. Prolific

Adjective/prəˈlɪf.ɪk/

বাংলা অর্থ: প্রচুর উৎপাদনশীল; অনেক কাজ, লেখা বা সৃষ্টি করে এমন।

English meaning: Producing a large amount of something, especially creative or intellectual work.

Synonyms: productive, fruitful, fertile. Antonym: unproductive.

Example: She was a prolific writer who published many books.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

8. Promulgate

Verb/ˈprɒm.əl.ɡeɪt/

বাংলা অর্থ: আইন, বিধি বা নীতি আনুষ্ঠানিকভাবে ঘোষণা বা জারি করা।

English meaning: To make a law, rule or official information publicly known by formally announcing it.

Synonyms: proclaim, announce, publish. Contrast: withhold, suppress.

Example: The government promulgated new regulations.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

9. Protrude

Verb/prəˈtruːd/

বাংলা অর্থ: বাইরে বেরিয়ে থাকা, কোনো পৃষ্ঠের বাইরে প্রসারিত হওয়া।

English meaning: To stick out or extend beyond a surface or boundary.

Synonyms: project, stick out, jut out. Antonyms: recede, retract.

Example: A nail was protruding from the wooden wall.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

10. Prudent

Adjective/ˈpruː.dənt/

বাংলা অর্থ: বিচক্ষণ, দূরদর্শী ও সতর্কভাবে সিদ্ধান্ত নেয় এমন।

English meaning: Acting with care and good judgement, especially to avoid unnecessary risk.

Synonyms: wise, sensible, judicious. Antonym: reckless.

Example: It is prudent to save some money for emergencies.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

11. Quell

Verb/kwel/

বাংলা অর্থ: দমন করা, শান্ত করা, কোনো গোলযোগ বা অনুভূতি থামিয়ে দেওয়া।

English meaning: To suppress, calm or put an end to something, especially a disturbance or fear.

Synonyms: suppress, subdue, pacify. Antonyms: incite, provoke.

Example: The police were called to quell the violent protest.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

12. Rancor

Noun/ˈræŋ.kər/

বাংলা অর্থ: গভীর ও দীর্ঘস্থায়ী ক্ষোভ, তিক্ত বিরাগ বা শত্রুতা।

English meaning: Deep, bitter and lasting resentment or hostility.

Synonyms: bitterness, resentment, animosity. Antonyms: goodwill, amity.

Example: Years of rancor divided the two families.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

13. Ratify

Verb/ˈræt.ɪ.faɪ/

বাংলা অর্থ: আনুষ্ঠানিকভাবে অনুমোদন বা অনুসমর্থন করা।

English meaning: To formally approve or confirm an agreement, treaty or decision so that it becomes officially valid.

Synonyms: approve, confirm, endorse. Antonyms: reject, repudiate.

Example: The parliament voted to ratify the treaty.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

14. Raze

Verb/reɪz/

বাংলা অর্থ: সম্পূর্ণ ধ্বংস করা, মাটির সঙ্গে মিশিয়ে দেওয়া।

English meaning: To completely destroy a building or structure.

Synonyms: demolish, level, destroy. Antonyms: build, construct.

Example: The old building was razed to make room for a new one.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

15. Rebut

Verb/rɪˈbʌt/

বাংলা অর্থ: যুক্তি বা প্রমাণ দিয়ে কোনো বক্তব্য খণ্ডন করা।

English meaning: To argue against a statement or claim, especially by presenting evidence that shows it is wrong.

Synonyms: refute, counter, contradict. Antonyms: support, confirm.

Example: The lawyer tried to rebut the witness's claim.

Dictionary: Cambridge · Merriam-Webster

Review and practice

  1. How is prudent different from phlegmatic?
  2. Which word means to announce a law or regulation formally?
  3. Explain the difference between ratify and rebut.
  4. Use posterity and precept in separate sentences.
  5. Which word describes deep, lasting resentment?
